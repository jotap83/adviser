# Núcleos e carteiras — extensão de atendimento

Mode: Operate. Code-led extension to the approved Adviser prototype. User asked where people join nuclei and how their client portfolios are assigned; approved the proposed implementation with “certo”. Preserve the existing identity and shared attendance access. No new direction selection or generated comp is owed.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Make ownership explicit at three levels: nucleus, client account and individual demand.
OWN-WORLD: Existing Adviser blue #293e91, orange #e94e1d, DM Sans, white tables and forms, 12px panels and 7px controls. No new assets or visual system.
STORY: Define a nucleus and its members, assign a client portfolio, open a demand with an editable owner, then inspect work by person or team.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing shell, sticky header and title. Settings presents an Equipe e carteiras panel with Pessoas/Núcleos tabs and one create action; the selected view exposes a table or inline edit form. Attendance places explicit portfolio filters above the existing funnel and queue. Management extends its existing nucleus breakdown with coordinator, client and member counts.
FORM: Extension of the inherited call-board and dossier form, seed 04f14614, not a new layout family. Native inputs and local table scrolling on mobile.
Signature interaction: selecting an existing client suggests its account owner for a new demand, while both the demand assignment and portfolio can subsequently change independently. No automatic reassignment of existing work.
Motion: inherited small state transitions; no new entrance motion or decorative animation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Required evidence
Desktop 1440×1000 and mobile 390×844: nuclei inline form, client portfolio edit, attendance filters, management breakdown. Functional checks cover membership, portfolio transfer, channel intake, existing-link ownership, unassigned work, persistence and shared visibility. No new raster assets.
